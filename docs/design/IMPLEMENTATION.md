## Coordinate and history contract

All three visible coordinates are spatial. Y is elevation in a neighborhood, not a calendar date. The pure [spatial model](../../src/domain/spatial.ts) derives regions from normalized primary tags, weighted-link propagation for untagged records, and graph-hub neighborhoods for remaining components. The cached `spatialWorldFor(nodes)` API in [field/layout.ts](../../src/world/field/layout.ts) exposes the actual positions, memberships, counts, and bounds.

`semantic-volume-v2` places stable topic centers at **Y = 150–230 units** and gives members deep local XYZ offsets. Before link-affinity adjustment, member sampling uses an X/Z radius of **35–65 units** and a vertical scale of **60–100 units**. X/Z neighborhood addresses remain stable. This depth belongs to the shared semantic anchors; it is not a camera trick or a renderer-only lift. Dates and the history origin remain absent from spatial layout inputs.

A selected note's **Spatial anchor** and **Neighborhood** come from that model. Its **History date** is separate. Changing only `temporalOrigin`, precision, or the history origin must preserve neighborhood membership and XYZ anchors. Dates still determine births, ghosts, and replay; `referenceDate` remains historical metadata.

World and Structure are treatments of the same spatial world. [Worker anchors](../../src/world/time/records.ts), [static fallback poses](../../src/world/nodes/staticPoses.ts), [GPU dust owners](../../src/world/dust/owners.ts), [the pose pipeline](../../src/world/time/usePhysicsPoses.ts), and [the field adapter](../../src/world/field/runtime.ts) agree on the versioned spatial model. Body geometry, media, picking, labels, saved links, agents, ground neighborhoods, cameras, and memory routes read that same world. Explicit `fieldAnchorXZ` pins remain placement inputs; transient integrator positions and velocities are not persisted. The reversible integer engine remains intact.

## Visible molecular volumes and relationship meaning

[Per-note molecular packets](../../src/world/field/molecules.ts) contain seeded three-dimensional interiors, asymmetric lobes, inclined filaments, and restrained halos. Core samples fill the interior by volume instead of collapsing into a bright central speck. Archetype, significance, and connection degree affect the bounded formation; the samples remain parts of one real note, not additional knowledge records.

[The field renderer](../../src/world/field/FieldLayer.tsx) now gives solid samples enough visible size and density at overview. Opaque, depth-writing shaded beads carry the volume; a separate restrained glow supplies atmosphere. Slower, smaller bounded curls move the beads and their bond endpoints together, and saved-link currents remain quiet. Original archetype bodies remain recognizable inside the formations, and the original text, files, images, video, and imported Gaussian splat scenes stay attached to their records.

There are two distinct connection meanings:

- **Local molecular bonds** are a bounded nearest-neighbor scaffold among samples inside one note. They are derived rendering geometry, never saved `NodeRecord.links`, inferred beliefs, or newly created knowledge relationships.
- **Saved inter-note links** remain the actual typed relationships and retain the Links key. [Helical particle flow](../../src/world/field/FieldThreads.tsx) follows those saved edges. Sharing a topic or sitting close in space does not create a saved link.

This revision changes presentation and shared spatial anchors. It does not rewrite existing content, original media, or typed links.

Real birth events in the selected history and actual selection engagement can emit bounded ground-particle ripples. There is no random background birth activity. Region labels are anchored in their actual three-dimensional data volumes, not left at the low ground. [GalaxyEnvironment](../../src/world/environment/GalaxyEnvironment.tsx) is explicitly **environmental art, not user data**. Its panorama keeps only a very slow steady drift. A fixed all-direction catalog of sparse colored stars supplies camera parallax and gentle long-period twinkle; individual stars no longer wander, the field does not rotate on its own, and the sky does not warp or pulse. Removing the old center/upper-hemisphere suppression, opaque-floor occlusion, and spatial height fog keeps the structure visible even when looking down across the world. The background stays dim enough for the actual note volumes to lead. Ambient time stops exactly with Motion paused and clamps large frame deltas on resume.

## Agents, labels, and navigation

[Visible agent bodies](../../src/world/agents/AgentLayer.tsx) target about **32 CSS pixels** across, with readable eyes, articulated fins, orbitals, and captions below their bodies. Slower local patrols, smaller secondary motion, and smoothly damped bounded separation keep them readable. The World guide is explicit UI rather than a fabricated note. Query agents represent actual saved queries; local matching and patrol routes visit real, born records. Motion paused holds their presentation exactly, and disabled queries report their actual state. The [Agents panel](../../src/hud/Agents.tsx) exposes real matches and controls; it does not claim background AI jobs or external-account activity.

The default [spatial camera](../../src/world/camera/spatialFrame.ts) is **low and oblique: azimuth 3.1, elevation 0.52 radians**. It fits actual three-dimensional bounds to make depth visible. District and note labels avoid agent caption/body areas, reminder banners, and each other. Overview names prioritize at most three districts on desktop and two on mobile; currently readable names remain until they leave view. Distant note representatives and placement sides stay stable instead of re-ranking every sample. During automatic orbit, ordinary note labels step aside; selected and hovered notes remain named, and ordinary close-up labels return when the orbit stops.

[Orbit scene](../../src/world/camera/orbitMotion.ts) is the primary scene action and is off until explicitly started. It eases into a roughly five-degree-per-second orbit around the visible target while preserving distance, with a small bounded elevation change. Stop orbit eases to rest; a manual drag, selection, navigation change, framing, or camera handoff interrupts immediately. Orbit does not advance history or change the ambient-motion preference. **Frame world** is the secondary action. The **Navigate** picker lists actual cluster names and note counts with accessible `Explore [name] cluster` buttons, so a region can be framed from any camera angle. The same menu groups Walk world, Fly freely, and Ride memories under Move freely; Escape closes it. Its bounded, scrollable layout retains access to long cluster lists.

**Walk world** follows the shared [ground function](../../src/world/camera/SpatialWalkRig.tsx); **Fly freely** explores XYZ; **Ride memories** can visit spatial memory positions in chronological sequence. Switching rendering treatment preserves navigation mode. The central chronology column, year-height ruler, and year landings are obsolete in the active presentation. The timeline supplies history, including births, reverse replay, Back to today, and Jump to first thought. The visible **Motion on / Motion paused** control governs agents, molecular flow, and the galaxy; pausing also stops an active camera orbit. History playback remains separate.

Populated worlds open to the full scene; conversation remains available through its explicit control. [Hud](../../src/hud/Hud.tsx) opens conversation by default only for an empty desktop world. Companion retains direct local Save, optional AI Send, originals, and failed-send recovery. Reflection writes dated notes. Sources retains files, folders, Obsidian, and source-reference imports. None of these imply authenticated external-account sync, proactive phone delivery, or automatic animated-3D reconstruction from an arbitrary photo/video.
