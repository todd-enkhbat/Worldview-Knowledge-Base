# Bundled folder cover photographs

These photographs were downloaded at 1600px, converted to WebP, and bundled locally. No third-party image request is made when displaying these covers. Photos are free to use under the Unsplash License: https://unsplash.com/license.

- `library.webp`: Jamie Taylor, “books organized in a shelf” — https://unsplash.com/photos/books-organized-in-a-shelf--9C3TMXwQjQ
- `forest.webp`: Anais Lauret, “Sunlight filtering through lush green forest canopy” — https://unsplash.com/photos/sunlight-filtering-through-lush-green-forest-canopy-tjgNyRSNFbs
- `architecture.webp`: “Ancient columns stand tall against the sky” — https://unsplash.com/photos/ancient-columns-stand-tall-against-the-sky-LEPmKgi8qNo

Earth and Milky Way presets reuse the existing locally bundled Worldview images and their existing attribution.
